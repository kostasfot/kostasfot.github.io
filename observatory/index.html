07 / TRUST & LIMITS

What this edition can—and cannot—tell you

    Travel value and airport accessibility

    Receipts, expenditure per trip/visit, length of stay and airport passenger charts remain in the local research product, but are withheld from this public edition pending publication clearance. This is not evidence that those measures are unimportant or unavailable at source.

    Inbound travellers, regional visits, accommodation check-ins and airport passengers count different events. Never add them together. Receipts are nominal spending, not GDP, profit or measured causal impact.
