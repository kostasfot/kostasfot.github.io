07 / TRAVEL VALUE

Spending and time spent beyond the check-in

Bank of Greece annual travel statistics, 2019–2025. National inbound travellers and Central Macedonia regional visits are separate populations—not hotel arrivals. A traveller may visit several regions. Regional figures exclude supplementary cruise data outside the Border Survey; national totals include it. No subregional spending estimates are invented.

These controls affect this section only. Full-year 2025 travel is available; it does not extend the shared accommodation period beyond 2024.

Expenditure = matching receipts ÷ travellers or visits; average stay = matching nights ÷ the same denominator. Source receipts are EUR millions and counts thousands, so expenditure converts the ratio to euros. YoY compares with the previous calendar year; recovery divides by the 2019 value and multiplies by 100. These are our calculations. Receipts are nominal, not adjusted for inflation, and do not measure profit, GDP or residents' wellbeing.

Source: Bank of Greece, Travel Services. See the final 2025 release and methodology notes. Calculations and original charts: Konstantinos Fotoglou. Independent analysis, not a Bank of Greece publication or endorsement.

08 / TRUST & LIMITS

What this edition can—and cannot—tell you

    Airport accessibility and maps

    Fraport airport charts and GISCO boundary maps remain outside this public edition pending the relevant reuse review. Their official sources are linked below; absence here does not mean the measures are unavailable at source.

    Inbound travellers, regional visits, accommodation check-ins and airport passengers count different events. Never add them together. Receipts are nominal spending, not GDP, profit or measured causal impact.
